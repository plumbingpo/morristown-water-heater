Kitchen Hot Water

Food-Service Hot Water Systems Built for the Rush

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest places to keep supplied with hot water. Every sink, sprayer, and dish cycle draws from the tank, a lunch rush can empty a small heater in minutes, and the dish machine depends on a booster to reach its final rinse temperature. Morristown Water Heater services, sizes, and repairs the tanks, tankless units, and booster heaters that keep both the kitchen and the restrooms supplied, and we plan service around your operating hours.

Temperature is where many restaurant hot water problems start. If the main heater can't keep up, handwashing sinks run lukewarm, the three-compartment sink falls below temperature, and the dish machine can't hit its final rinse at peak service. We verify temperatures at the tank, the booster, and the fixtures and log every visit in written reports that inspectors and landlords can review.

We work around your business, not the other way around. We coordinate every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. Off-hours installation is available when needed, and downtime stays short so the kitchen opens on time.

Code & Inspections

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. We inspect venting and draft, verify proper combustion air, check relief valves and their discharge lines, confirm expansion tanks are charged correctly, and set mixing valves so water can be stored hot enough to control bacteria while reaching the tap at a safe temperature. We pull permits and schedule inspections where required. Findings go into a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

Regular professional service protects your people and your property, and acting on expert recommendations keeps minor issues from becoming emergencies. It also builds a paper trail. We maintain service logs, temperature records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and inspectors that you've done your due diligence.

After-Hours Service

After-Hours Commercial Water Heater Response

A commercial water heater failure starts costing money the minute it happens. Dish rooms stall, salon chairs empty, guests call the front desk, and tenants start complaining. Our commercial line gets a technician rolling quickly for emergency repairs throughout Morristown and the surrounding area, and planned work can be done after hours to limit downtime.

Failed gas valves, burned-out elements, ignition faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks, stalled recirculation pumps, tankless lockouts: we see all of it on tank, tankless, and boiler-fed systems. The priority is pinpointing the fault and getting hot water back as quickly as possible, often by isolating the failed heater so the others carry the load. Once it's running, we set up a maintenance schedule so it doesn't happen again, and each response is fully documented for your facility files.

Maintenance Programs

Commercial Water Heater Maintenance Programs

A maintenance program from Morristown Water Heater gets built around your equipment, how the building uses hot water, and your budget.
